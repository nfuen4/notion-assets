## Master framework: the spine behind every post

Every carousel maps back to one stage of the account's 7-step model:

| # | Step | What it means |
|---|------|---------------|
| 1 | Define the Problem | A clear problem statement: *what is it, and why solve it?* |
| 2 | Split into Small Parts | Logic/issue trees, 5 Whys, bucketing |
| 3 | Analyse Each Part | Hypothesis-driven, data- and evidence-led |
| 4 | Create Insight | Pull out the "gold nugget" pattern |
| 5 | Craft a Story | A structured narrative that sells the idea |
| 6 | Make a Decision | Options, trade-offs, stakeholder buy-in |
| 7 | Take Bold Action | Roadmap and execution plan |

The posts group into three concept buckets:

- **A. Thinking clearly:** steps 1–4
- **B. Communicating and influencing:** steps 5–6
- **C. Market and customer strategy:** applying the thinking to growth

## Post-by-post outline (most recent first)

### 1. Reframing: "Feeling stuck? Change your perspective" (~0 days ago) · Bucket A
**Hook:** Being stuck on a problem is often a perspective problem.
**Concept:** Reframing means changing the lens you use on a challenge. The post gives four methods:
1. **Shift the Context.** Look at the resources you have, not your limits.
   - Prompt: *"What can we achieve with what we have?"*
   - Example: a small business with no ad budget leans on organic social media.
2. **Reinterpret the Problem.** Look at the challenge from other angles.
   - Prompt: *"What else could this problem mean?"*
   - Example: falling sales can signal that customer needs are changing.
3. **Zoom Out.** Look at the bigger system.
   - Prompt: *"Is this goal incremental or strategic?"*
   - Example: employee turnover can point to a deeper culture problem.
4. *(4th method not visible; likely a "zoom in" or "flip the question" move)*

### 2. Clarity: "The biggest edge for strategists" (~1 day ago) · Bucket A
**Hook:** Vague goals and endless meetings kill progress, and clarity fixes them.
**Concept:** The 7-step decision-making method, walked through in order:
1. **Define the problem.** Frame the challenge before trying to solve it.
2. **Split it into parts.** Use issue trees or the 5 Whys to find root causes.
3. **Analyse each part.** Use evidence, data and trends to find bottlenecks and gaps.
4. *(inferred)* Create insight → 5. Craft a story → 6. Make a decision → 7. Take bold action

### 3. Market awareness: "Only 3% of prospects are ready to buy now" (~2 days ago) · Bucket C
**Hook:** Most of your audience isn't ready to buy, so match your message to where each person is.
**Concept:** Eugene Schwartz's 5 Stages of Awareness, with a content type for each stage:
1. **Unaware.** Goal: spark curiosity. Formats: relatable videos, emotional content, surprising stats.
2. **Problem-Aware.** Goal: show empathy and teach. Formats: how-to guides, educational posts, case studies.
3. **Solution-Aware.** Goal: make your solution category look like the best choice. Formats: comparison guides, webinars, whitepapers.
4. *(inferred)* **Product-Aware.** Goal: stand out from alternatives. Formats: demos, testimonials, reviews.
5. *(inferred)* **Most Aware.** Goal: close. Formats: offers, guarantees, a direct call to action.

### 4. Influence: "Confusion hurts any momentum you've gained" (~3 days ago) · Bucket B
**Hook:** Good strategy fails if nobody understands it.
**Concept:** Three ways strategic thinking builds influence:
1. **Synthesise a clear insight.** Zoom out to find patterns, then say it in *"one bold sentence that cuts through the noise."*
2. **Use persuasive storytelling.** Structure: **Challenge → Big Idea → Action**, told from the audience's point of view.
3. **Guide the decision.** Set out the choices clearly and show the trade-offs. Being open about trade-offs builds trust.

## Recurring patterns

**Content format**
- Carousels throughout: a hook slide, then one slide per method or step, then a recap or CTA.
- A numbered list of 3, 4 or 7 items in every post.
- Each point gets a **name + guiding question + business example**. This "prompt question" is the signature device.

**Hook formulas**
- Pain point: "Feeling stuck…", "Vague goals and endless meetings…"
- Surprising stat: "Only 3% of prospects…"
- Bold claim: "Clarity is the biggest edge…", "Confusion hurts momentum…"

**Core themes**
1. **Perspective shifting:** reframing, zooming out/in
2. **Structured problem solving:** define → decompose → analyse
3. **Insight to story to decision:** turning analysis into influence
4. **Audience-centric marketing:** awareness stages, meeting people where they are

**Borrowed or credited frameworks**
- Eugene Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising* (stages of awareness)
- McKinsey-style issue trees
- Toyota's 5 Whys
- Story structure: Challenge → Big Idea → Action

**Funnel logic of the feed**
Free frameworks in the carousels → the 7-step model as the connecting thread → the paid toolkit/course (50 models) and a 21-day email series at overnightstrategist.com.
