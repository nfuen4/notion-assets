The 5 October pace cliff

The workload roughly doubles the day Chapter 1 starts

Chapter P gets nearly three 55-minute sessions per section. Chapter 1 gets barely one — and 1.3, 1.6 and 1.7 are the first genuinely abstract sections in the book. The schedule silently assumes functions are half as hard as exponent rules.
