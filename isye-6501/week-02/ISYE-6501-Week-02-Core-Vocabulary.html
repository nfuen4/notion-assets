K-Fold Cross-Validation Data Splits Click or press Enter to reveal
Rotate validation across k slices

Splitting data into k equal slices (e.g., 5 slices) and taking turns holding out 1 slice for validation while training on the other k − 1 slices.
Having 5 practice tests and rotating which one you test yourself on.
Clustering (Unsupervised) Clustering Click or press Enter to reveal
No pre-existing labels

Grouping similar data points together when you do not have pre-existing category labels.
Dumping a box of assorted Lego bricks on the floor and sorting them into piles that look alike.
Centroid (Cluster Center) Clustering Click or press Enter to reveal
Center of gravity

The “center of gravity” (average point) at the middle of a cluster in K-Means.
Placing a regional distribution warehouse right in the geographic center of the cities it serves.
